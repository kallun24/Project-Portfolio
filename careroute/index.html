The opportunity

Everyday care administration can be simpler.

CareRoute was shaped around practical issues observed in domiciliary care, where good care can still depend on repetitive paper and manual processes.

01

Weekly rota distribution

Rotas are repeatedly prepared and sent to care workers, creating extra administration and a risk of older versions being used.

02

Manual working hours

Care workers may still need to report hours separately even though scheduled and completed visit activity could provide a clearer record.

03

Paper and scattered information

Rotas, care plans and visit information can sit in different places, increasing the effort needed to find what matters during a busy day.

04

Limited live visibility

Managers cannot always see the day's operational picture in one place without calls, messages or later reconciliation.

05

Rota changes create more messages

A schedule adjustment can mean another document, another message and another check that everybody has the latest version.

06

Route planning sits with the carer

Care workers still need to make sense of locations and visit sequences while managing appointment times and care responsibilities.

One connected workflow

Digitising the processes care teams already do.

CareRoute is designed to complement existing care procedures, not replace professional judgement, official care instructions or safeguarding processes.

▦

Digital rota

Publish the weekly rota once and make the current version available directly to the care worker.

♥

Care information

Place the relevant care plan or approved visit information alongside each scheduled client.

⌖

Route support

Open individual addresses or the day's route directly in mapping tools from the mobile workflow.

✓

Visit progress

Give carers a simple way to see what is scheduled, what is completed and what still needs attention.

◷

Working hours

Calculate scheduled and completed care time so hours do not have to be reconstructed manually.

↻

Central updates

Change the rota centrally so the care worker sees the most recent authorised version.

!

Conflict warnings

Highlight overlapping visits or missing information for review rather than leaving issues hidden in a document.

▤

Management dashboard

Bring staff activity, visit progress, hours and exceptions into one operational view.

Care worker journey

A care day, clearly organised.

Step 1Rota published

The latest authorised rota becomes available in the app.

Step 2Open today's visits

The carer sees the schedule in a clear daily sequence.

Step 3Review care info

Relevant approved care information is available with the visit.

Step 4Navigate & deliver

Open directions, follow the official care plan and complete the visit.

Step 5Progress updates

Completion and hours become visible without another manual report.
