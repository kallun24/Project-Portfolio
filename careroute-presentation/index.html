Why CareRoute

Less administration around the care that already matters.

CareRoute is designed around recurring operational issues in domiciliary care, including paper rotas, manual working-hour reporting and information spread across different places.

01

Digital weekly rota

Publish the latest authorised rota once and make it available directly to the care worker.

02

Visible working hours

Scheduled and completed care time can be seen centrally, reducing separate manual reporting.

03

Care information together

Approved care information can sit alongside the relevant scheduled visit.

04

Live operational view

Managers can see visit progress and exceptions from one dashboard.

05

Central rota changes

Update the rota centrally so carers can see the current authorised version.

06

Practical route support

Move from the day's schedule to navigation and the next visit within one workflow.

How it works

A care day, clearly organised.

Step 1Rota published

The latest approved schedule is made available digitally.

Step 2Today's visits

The care worker sees the day in a clear sequence.

Step 3Care information

Relevant approved information is available with the visit.

Step 4Navigate & deliver

Open directions and follow the employer's official care process.

Step 5Progress visible

Completion and working time can feed the management view.

For coordinators and managers

See the day from one central dashboard.

CareRoute's organisation view is intended to bring scheduled visits, completion progress, working hours and exceptions together.
