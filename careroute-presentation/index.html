Why CareRoute

Less administration around the care that already matters.

The product is designed around recurring operational issues in domiciliary care, especially paper rotas, manual reporting and information spread across different places.

01

Digital weekly rota

Publish the latest authorised rota once and make it available directly to the care worker instead of repeatedly sending documents.

02

Visible working hours

Scheduled and completed care time can be seen centrally, reducing the need to reconstruct or separately report hours.

03

Care information together

Approved care information can sit alongside the relevant scheduled visit so carers spend less time searching.

04

Live operational view

Managers can see visit progress and exceptions from one dashboard rather than relying only on calls and later reconciliation.

05

Central rota changes

Update the rota centrally so the current version is visible without sending another amended document.

06

Practical route support

Care workers can move from the day's schedule to navigation and the next visit within one simple mobile workflow.

How it works

A care day, clearly organised.

Step 1Rota published

The latest approved schedule is made available digitally.

Step 2Today's visits

The care worker sees the day in a clear sequence.

Step 3Care information

Relevant approved information is available with the visit.

Step 4Navigate & deliver

Open directions and follow the employer's official care process.

Step 5Progress visible

Completion and working time can feed the management view.

For coordinators and managers

See the day from one central dashboard.

CareRoute's organisation view is intended to reduce repetitive administration by bringing scheduled visits, completion progress, working hours and exceptions together.
